# Lask's Garden 🎮🌱🎵

**Lask's Garden** is a peaceful gardening simulation game that also functions as a generative music composition tool. Each plant you place creates unique sounds based on its type and position, allowing players to “compose” an evolving piece of music simply by designing their garden.

---

## 🧠 Core Concept

As players interact with the environment and plant different vegetation — flowers, bushes, pots, and grids — musical parameters change dynamically. Each object affects the pitch, timbre, timing, or pan of sound events. The garden becomes an intuitive, creative musical playground.

---

## ⚙️ Technologies Used

- **Unity** (game engine)
- **C#** (scripting language)
- **uRTcmix** (real-time audio synthesis library)
- **RTcmix** (C-based sound synthesis + processing engine embedded via Unity integration)

---

## 🎹 Instruments Used (RTcmix)

The game utilizes a variety of RTcmix instruments to simulate musical textures:

- `WAVETABLE` — basic wave-based synthesis
- `MBLOWHOLE` — modeled wind instrument
- `MBRASS` — brass-like synthesis with rich timbre
- (others optionally include: `STRUM2`, `FMINST`, etc.)

Each plant type maps to a category of instruments and musical roles (melody, harmony, bass, etc.), and parameters like reverb, pan, pitch, and rhythm are determined by both **plant tone** (warm/cool/neutral) and **in-game spatial position**.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Click on the garden space to place a plant from the UI.
- Each plant plays a sound (generated by uRTcmix) on placement.
- Sounds evolve based on the number, type, and position of plants.
- Players explore and compose music passively or actively by shaping their garden.
